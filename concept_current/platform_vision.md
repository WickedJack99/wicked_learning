# Platform Vision

Learning Worlds is an open-source learning platform focused on intrinsic
motivation and exploratory learning.

The platform should help learners explore knowledge through configurable
worlds, spatial maps, MapAssets, characters and activity routes. It must not use
points, streak pressure, public rankings or status rewards as the main reason to
return.

The product supports:

- **Autonomy:** learners choose meaningful paths and can retry without shame.
- **Competence:** feedback explains what improved and what to inspect next.
- **Relatedness:** cooperation, learner messages and shared reflection support
  connection without comparison.
- **Wellbeing:** learning loops should feel inviting, active and humane.

MapAssets are the learner-facing places on a map. Their shape and imagery are
domain-specific; a MapAsset may be an organ, location, instrument, concept or
decorative layer. Focusable MapAssets contain activity routes, while visual-only
MapAssets help form the map without becoming interactive content.

The current platform already includes configurable maps and MapAssets, graph
activities, learner progress, journals and feedback, groups and shared tasks,
items and tools, competence support, extensive Settings administration and
replaceable presentation/media layers.

Future collaboration should grow from shared tasks into meaningful projects. A
group may receive a competence-appropriate brief, divide feasible work, review
and revise contributions, and present a resulting system, artifact, map or
activity path. Progress should remain understandable without becoming a
leaderboard.

The platform stays generic so deployments can change terminology, visuals,
characters, media and narrative framing without rewriting core learning logic.
